How can we help?

Find answers to the most common questions below. If you can't find what you're looking for, reach out directly.

Tickets & Booking

Browse events on the home page, click the event you want to attend, then select your ticket type and quantity. You'll be taken through a short checkout. No account is required to buy tickets.

Your tickets are sent to the email address you provided at checkout. You can show the ticket directly from your email at the venue - no printing needed.

Refund policies are set by the event organizer, not entri. Check the event details page for the organizer's policy before you purchase. If you believe you're owed a refund, contact the organizer directly.

If an organizer cancels their event, attendees are notified by email. Whether refunds are issued depends on the organizer's stated policy. entri does not process refunds on behalf of organizers.

For Organizers

Sign in to your account, open the dashboard, and click "New event." Fill in your event details - title, date, venue, and description - then add your ticket tiers and publish when you're ready.

Yes. When setting up your event you can add multiple ticket tiers - early bird, VIP, general admission, or anything else - each with its own name, price, and quantity cap.

Your dashboard shows a real-time summary of sales, revenue, and remaining capacity for every active event. No manual exports or spreadsheets needed.

Open the event from your dashboard and go to the attendee list. You can scan ticket QR codes or manually mark attendees as checked in as they arrive.
